Akkineni Naga Chaitanya (born 23 November 1986) is an Indian actor and entrepreneur who works predominantly in Telugu cinema. He achieved his career breakthrough with the romantic drama Ye Maaya Chesave (2010), earning his first Filmfare Award for Best Actor – Telugu nomination.
